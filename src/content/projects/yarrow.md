Yarrow's kitchen is a symphony of motion, heat, and precision. We spent time documenting the service without getting in the way, opting for a fly-on-the-wall approach.

This allowed us to capture the genuine intensity and passion of the team. The resulting imagery is raw, unpolished in the best way, and completely true to the heart of the restaurant.
