The old Confluence website had been sitting untouched since 2022. It had a template design, copy that could've belonged to any therapy practice in the country, and a navigation with six competing links that made it hard to land anywhere with confidence.

None of that matched who Elly actually is — direct, a little funny, genuinely invested in the people she works with.

We started by stripping everything back. One clear idea per page. Copy written to sound like a real person runs this practice, because one does. A layout that creates room to breathe instead of filling every pixel with information. And a structure with one job: move someone from *I've been meaning to look into therapy* to actually booking an appointment.

The result is a site that feels like walking into the office — warm, unhurried, and confident in what it's offering.
