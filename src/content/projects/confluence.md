For this portrait series, we wanted to step away from the sterile studio environment and bring the subjects into a warm, inviting space. The greenhouse provided the perfect backdrop for the team's vibrant personalities to shine.

Our focus was on fostering a comfortable atmosphere, allowing for authentic expressions and genuine connections. The resulting portraits are an honest reflection of each individual's unique character.
